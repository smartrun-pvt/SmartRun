## 1. Forensic Diagnosis: Why Domain Rating (DR) is Currently 0

Ahrefs calculates **Domain Rating (DR)** on a logarithmic scale from 0 to 100 based strictly on:
1. **The number of unique domains that have at least one DOFOLLOW link to your website.**
2. **The DR values of those linking domains.**
3. **How many other sites those referring domains link to** (link equity dilution).

### The Critical Bottleneck in smartrun.in's Backlink Profile:
- **399 Total Backlinks, but only 4% are Dofollow**:
  - Out of 399 links, approximately **16 links** are followed; the remaining ~383 links are `rel="nofollow"` or ignored bot scrapers (DNS lookup portals, automated domain evaluation bots, web stat aggregators).
  - Nofollow links pass **zero link equity / PageRank** in Google and Ahrefs algorithms.
- **Diluted & Low-Authority Referring Domains**:
  - The few domains currently linking have a DR of 0 to 5. Zero tier-1 (DR 70+) followed links currently point to `smartrun.in`.
- **Missing Brand & Keyword Anchor Text Profiles**:
  - The anchor texts are largely empty URLs (`https://smartrun.in/`) or generic scrapes without localized Kolkata electrical and construction keyword clustering.

As highlighted in the Ahrefs reference guide:
> *"Use the '404 not found' filter to find highly linked-to broken pages on any website and kickstart a broken link building campaign."*

### Server-Side Implementation in SmartRun:
1. **Crawler-Friendly 404 Recovery**:
   - `server.ts` handles unmatched crawler hits gracefully by serving an HTML recovery payload containing dofollow breadcrumbs and direct links to `/electrical`, `/construction`, `/technicians`, and `/sitemap`.
2. **301 Permanent Redirects for Historic URLs**:
   - Any legacy URLs (e.g. from previous store versions) should be permanently 301 redirected to `/electrical` or `/product/:id` so inbound link equity is never lost.
3. **Internal Linking Anchor Architecture**:
   - Product detail pages interlink related category items with explicit, keyword-rich anchor text (`Buy Polycab Wire in Kolkata`, `Schneider 32A MCB`) instead of generic `"click here"` or raw URLs.
